# 1. Why did you leave your previous job?

I left my previous job because I was looking for better learning and growth opportunities, especially in communication and English skills. Although I was earning a good salary, I realized that long-term career growth and continuous learning are more important to me than salary. I believe this is the right stage of my life to learn, grow, and develop myself professionally.


# 2. Was there any problem with your previous company or job?

No, there was no major problem with the company. My decision was mainly based on my personal and professional growth. My previous role was focused on collections, but I wanted an opportunity where I could learn more, improve my communication skills, and develop myself professionally.


# 3. Why is learning more important to you than salary?

I believe salary is important, but at this stage of my career, learning and professional growth are equally important to me. If I continuously improve my skills and gain valuable experience, I believe better career opportunities and financial growth will naturally follow in the future.


# 4. What were you looking for in your next job?

I am looking for an opportunity where I can learn, grow, improve my communication skills, and take on new challenges. I want to work in an environment where I can develop professionally and build a stable, long-term career.
